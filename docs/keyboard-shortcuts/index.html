Keyboard Shortcuts

Every hotkey and touch trigger for running Motion Poster.

Shortcuts

KeyAction
FEnter or exit fullscreen
QToggle Quick Controls
CToggle Customize Appearance
EToggle Edit Poster Text
AToggle Add/Remove Names
RReset appearance to defaults (while Customize is open)
WStart the wireless remote
? or /Show the help menu
EscClose the open panel, or exit fullscreen
Hold \Factory reset: wipe all local data

Shortcuts are ignored while you are typing in a text field.

Touch Triggers

  • Open the menu: tap and hold the top-right corner of the screen.
  • Remove a host: tap and hold a name on the poster.
