Troubleshooting & FAQ

Fixes for common issues, plus how your data is stored and how to start over.

My Changes Disappeared

Settings are stored in your browser for this site. They will not carry over to a different browser, a different computer, a private or incognito window, or after you clear site data. Set up the poster on the same browser and computer you will use at the event.

Where Is My Data Stored?

All customizations, including logos and QR images, are stored locally in your browser. Motion Poster has no accounts, and nothing you type is uploaded to a database. The only network calls are loading the page and fonts, optional QR code generation, and the local wireless remote connection.

The Poster Left Fullscreen

Browsers require a user action to enter fullscreen, so after a reload press F (or click when prompted) to go back. Press Esc only when you want to leave.

My QR Code Will Not Scan

  • Use a high-contrast pair of colors, with the dark color as the code itself.
  • Avoid very small sizes, low-resolution uploads, or busy overlay icons.
  • Check that the URL is complete and begins with https://.

The Remote Will Not Connect

Confirm both devices are on the same network, keep the poster page open, and try a phone hotspot if venue Wi-Fi blocks device-to-device traffic. See Remote Control tips.

Animation Is Choppy

Reduce the particle Count, lower the frame rate cap, or pick a lighter theme. Close other tabs and apps on the display computer.

Start Over (Factory Reset)

Press and hold \ to wipe all local data and restore defaults. This removes your names, text, logos, and QR codes, so only do it when you mean to.
