Customizing Appearance

Tune particle physics, host list layout, colors, backdrop strength, and performance to fit your venue.

Open the Section

Press C (or open Quick Controls with Q and expand Customize Appearance). Changes apply live, so you can adjust while the poster is on screen.

Particle Dynamics

SliderWhat it does
CountHow many particles are on screen (0 to 500).
WindinessHow often gusts of wind occur.
Fall SpeedOverall drift speed of particles.
Tumble SpeedHow quickly particles rotate as they fall.
IntensityHow strongly gusts move particles and swaying frame elements.

Set Count to 0 for a calm, static poster. Lower values are also kinder to older computers.

Host List Layout

  • Layout mode: Justified, Centered, or Columns.
  • Size: Scale the text from 0.5x to 2x. The list also auto-fits so 5 names and 50 names both look right.
  • Max Width: Constrain how wide the list can grow.
  • Vertical and Horizontal Spacing: Adjust margins from the frame.

Colors and Legibility

  • Swatches: Each theme offers curated palettes. Pick one with a click.
  • Custom colors: Use the color picker to match your brand's background and accent colors.
  • Text Backdrop Strength: Adds separation behind names so they stay readable over busy backgrounds.

Show and Hide Elements

Independently toggle the logo, event title, date, host list, QR code areas, and the theme frame or background animations. Hide what you do not need for a cleaner look.

Performance Settings

  • Frame rate limiter: Cap animation at 30, 60, 90, or 120 FPS to save battery or match a high-refresh display.
  • Smooth transitions: Turn UI animations on or off.
  • Auto-fullscreen recovery: Disable this if you do not want the poster to re-enter fullscreen after a refresh.

The Status area shows a live FPS counter, screen resolution, fullscreen session timer, and wake lock status so you can verify everything is healthy.

Reset to Defaults

While the Customize section is open, press R to reset appearance settings to the theme defaults. Your names and text are not affected.
