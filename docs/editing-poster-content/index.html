Editing Poster Content

Set your event text, upload a logo, and add QR codes for registration or donations.

Edit Poster Text

Press E to open Edit Poster Text. You can change the organization name, top label, event title, subtitle, date, and the heading above the host list.

Upload a Logo

Upload a logo from the content editor. PNG or SVG with a transparent background looks best, and horizontal logos fit the banner most naturally. Keep images under 2 MB for smooth performance and reliable saving.

Add QR Codes

The poster has two QR code areas, left and right, which you can use independently (for example, tickets on the left and donations on the right). There are two ways to fill them:

Option 1: Generate from a URL

  1. Paste your link into the QR field for that side.
  2. Click Generate. The code is created for you and applied to the poster.

Generated codes are made by QR Maker, so this option needs an internet connection at the moment you generate. Once applied, the image is stored in your browser and works offline.

Option 2: Upload an image

Upload any standard QR image (PNG, JPG, and so on). Use a square, high-contrast, high-resolution image so it scans reliably from across the room.

Customize the style

For generated codes, open Customize QR Code Style to change the corner style (square, rounded, circle, leaf, beveled), foreground and background colors, a transparent background, and an overlay icon such as a link, Wi-Fi, map pin, or social network logo.

Always test-scan your code with a phone before the event.

Everything Is Saved

Text, logos, and QR codes persist in your browser across refreshes and restarts. See Troubleshooting for how storage works and how to clear it.
