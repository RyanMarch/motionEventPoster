Getting Started

Put a living poster on your screen in five minutes: launch, go fullscreen, pick a theme, and add your hosts.

Launch the Poster

Open motionposter.ryanmarch.me/app in a current version of Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox on the computer that is connected to your display. There is nothing to install and no account to create.

The poster starts with a sample theme and placeholder names so you can see what it looks like right away. Everything you change is saved in the browser automatically.

Five-Minute Setup

  1. Open the controls. Press Q to open Quick Controls. No keyboard? Tap and hold the top-right corner of the screen.
  2. Pick a theme. Open Customize Appearance (C) and choose a theme. See Themes.
  3. Add your text and logo. Open Edit Poster Text (E) to set your organization, event title, subtitle, and date. See Editing Poster Content.
  4. Add your hosts. Open Add/Remove Names (A). See Managing Hosts.
  5. Go fullscreen. Press F. This also keeps the screen awake.

Connect to a Display

Plug your computer into the TV or projector, set it to extend or mirror your screen, and drag the browser window to that display before pressing F. Motion Poster is tuned for 1080p (FHD) and 1440p (QHD) displays and scales to larger screens.

Want to run it from a chair instead of the display computer? Use the wireless remote.
