Managing Hosts

Add, remove, and restore names on the host committee list, from the display or from your phone.

Add Names

Press A to open Add/Remove Names, type a name, and press Enter. Duplicates are detected for you. The first time you add a name, the sample names are replaced by your own list.

Remove Names

  • From the list: Choose a name in the panel and remove it.
  • From the display: Tap and hold any name on the poster to remove it. No keyboard needed.

Restore a Removed Name

Removed a name by mistake? Recently removed names appear in a Recently Removed list in the same panel. Click one to put it back.

Layout and Sizing

Names automatically scale to fit your screen. To change how they are arranged (Justified, Centered, or Columns), see Customizing Appearance.

Manage from Your Phone

With the wireless remote connected you can add and remove names from another device while the poster stays on the big screen.
