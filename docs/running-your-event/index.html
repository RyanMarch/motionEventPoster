Running Your Event

A pre-event checklist and the reliability features that keep the poster up all night.

Before Doors Open

  1. Load the poster on the display computer and set your theme, text, logo, QR codes, and names.
  2. Press F to enter fullscreen on the correct display.
  3. Scan your QR codes with a phone from where guests will stand.
  4. Pair your phone or tablet as a remote and make one test edit.
  5. Plug the computer into power and turn off any system sleep or screensaver you can.

Built-In Reliability

  • Wake lock: Fullscreen mode asks the computer to stay awake so the screen does not dim or sleep. Older browsers fall back to a silent video to achieve the same effect.
  • Auto-fullscreen recovery: If the page reloads or the power blinks, the poster remembers it was fullscreen and makes it easy to return.
  • Everything is saved: Settings, names, logos, and QR codes live in your browser's storage and survive restarts.

Do I Need the Internet?

Once the poster page has loaded, it keeps running on its own. Internet is only needed to load the page the first time, and to generate a new QR code from a URL. The wireless remote only needs both devices on the same local network. For the most predictable result, load everything at home or the office before you travel.

During the Event

  • Use the remote to add late-arriving hosts or fix a typo without leaving your seat.
  • If the screen is busy, lower the particle Count or raise Text Backdrop Strength.
  • Watch the FPS counter in Quick Controls. If it dips, lower the frame rate cap or reduce particles.
