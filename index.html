Make it yours

Your title, your logo, your donation QR

Type the event title, subtitle, and date. Drop in a logo. Add a QR code for tickets or donations by uploading an image or pasting a link and letting Motion Poster make a styled one.

  • Left and right QR areas, independently toggled

Hosts & sponsors

A host list that always fits

Five names or fifty, the list rescales itself to fit. Tap and hold a name on the display to remove it, handy for a typo or a last-minute change, and undo to quickly revert.

  • Justified, centered, or column layouts
  • Smart font scaling with max width and spacing controls
  • Full undo history for removed names

Wireless remote

Full control from your phone or tablet

Pair an iPhone, iPad, or laptop to start customizing on the spot. Changes show up on the display instantly, so you can fix a typo or add a late arrival from anywhere in the room.

Pairing works over the network with nothing to install. No internet connection needed. Venue Wi-Fi works, and so does a smartphone hotspot.

  1. 1
    Press W on the poster
  2. 2
    Scan the QR code or enter the pairing code
  3. 3
    Edit everything live from any phone or tablet

Built for the big night

Set it and forget it

Stays awake

Fullscreen mode blocks screensavers and sleep, so the display stays bright the whole event.

Recovers on its own

If the power flickers or the browser restarts, your text and settings come back and it relaunches fullscreen.

Runs without the internet

It runs entirely in the browser, so the display never freezes or goes blank if the venue loses internet.

Tunable physics

Particle count, speed, margins, and frame rate are all sliders, so it looks right on a 4K display or a tablet on a table.

Keyboard or touch

Hotkeys for everything, or tap and hold the top-right corner when there is no keyboard connected.

Private by design

No accounts and no database. Your logos, names, and settings stay in your own browser.
