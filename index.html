Make it yours

Your name, your logo, your QR codes

Set the event title, subtitle, and date in seconds. Upload a logo, then add QR codes for tickets or donations, either by uploading an image or by pasting a link and letting Motion Poster generate a styled code for you.

  • Left and right QR areas, independently toggled
  • Rounded, circle, leaf, or beveled QR corners with brand colors and icon overlays
  • Everything saves in your browser and survives refreshes and restarts

Hosts & sponsors

A host list that always fits

Five names or fifty, the list rescales itself to your screen. Add a guest as they arrive, tap and hold to remove one, and bring back a recently removed name with a single click.

  • Justified, centered, or column layouts
  • Smart font scaling with max width and spacing controls
  • A Recently Removed safety net

Wireless remote

Run the room from your seat

Press W, scan the code with your phone, and you are in control. Swap themes, fix a typo, or add a late arrival without walking to the display computer. It is just a web page, so there is no app to download.

  1. 1
    Press W on the poster
  2. 2
    Scan the QR code or enter the pairing code
  3. 3
    Edit everything live from any phone or tablet

Both devices need to be on the same network. A phone hotspot works great.

Set it and forget it

Stays awake

Fullscreen mode holds a wake lock so the screen never dims, with a silent-video fallback for older browsers.

Recovers on its own

If the page refreshes or the power blinks, your settings, names, and fullscreen state come right back.

Runs without the internet

After the first load the poster needs no connection, and the remote works on any local network.

Tunable physics

Dial in particle count, wind, fall speed, and frame rate to fit your display and your venue.

Keyboard or touch

Hotkeys for everything, or tap and hold the corner when there is no keyboard in sight.

Private by design

No accounts and no database. Your logos, names, and settings stay in your own browser.
